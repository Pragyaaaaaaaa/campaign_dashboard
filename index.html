About the data
  • Elections: 2017 and 2022 local results, plus the March 2026 House vote by district. 2022 ward chair, member, women and Dalit women results list every candidate with votes for 752 local levels (collected from Kantipur's results pages). Mayor/chair and deputy are published for the top two candidates only in 2022; 2017 lists up to four per post and the rest as one line.
  • People: Census 2021 by sex and age for every ward; 2022 registered voters per local level.
  • Map: real boundaries, 9.46 million building footprints, health facilities and road lines from Galli Maps. Routes follow the road-line tiles.
  • Visits: demo status to start with. Visits, notes and contributor name you enter are saved in this browser only.

Sources: Nepal Local Election Atlas (Election Commission, Kantipur), National Statistics Office, Galli Maps.
